# KD102 v0.001 · release notes

September 26, 2026

## Guest experience

- Added a standalone companion letter from Chervil that introduces KD102 as an independent concept, not an official Tauck app, and invites feedback throughout the journey.
- Updated the silent interactive tutorial. The suitcase rolls in and waits for “Let’s Unpack”; the instruction card waits for “Let our journey begin.” Pause, back and forward, chapters, speed, captions and fullscreen remain available.
- Added a nine-second closing card: “Your journey is packed. Now let’s go experience it,” with a prompt to find “Help Shape What Comes Next” under More.
- Created a seven-page illustrated Quick Guide with KD102 tutorial visuals, suitable for reading or printing.

## App

- Renamed the feedback panel in More to “Help Shape What Comes Next.” Its prompts now ask what delighted guests, what could be simpler and what they wish the app could do. Guests can return to update their thoughts during the journey.
- Linked the standalone tutorial, letter and Quick Guide from Info. The in-app video uses the new silent cut.
- Kept the KD101 phone Home Screen icon for KD102.

## Verification and limits

- The production build, TypeScript check and 92-page itinerary content check passed. The letter and booklet were rendered and visually reviewed; the video is silent and 1:56 long.
- Tour 102 schedule details, Chervil’s official welcome letter and contact details remain pending confirmation. The feedback API needs site functions and Blobs, so posting, editing and deleting must be checked on the test site before a live release.
